# 🕹️ Zoology Schmoology Arcade

Welcome to **Zoology Schmoology**, an interactive, retro-style arcade experience where wildlife education meets fast-paced gameplay! 🧪🐸

Test your knowledge, visual perception, and identification skills across a variety of interactive mini-games. Designed to make learning biology fun and immersive, this platform challenges you to spot, track, and identify diverse species—from deep-sea rays and cryptic insects to nocturnal bats and rare birds.

### 🌟 Key Features
* **Interactive Learning:** Hands-on games designed to build real-world species identification skills.
* **Retro Arcade Aesthetic:** Cyberpunk visuals paired with classic arcade styling.
* **Diverse Wildlife Modules:** Master identification across 10+ distinct animal groups including lepidoptera, marine life, arachnids, and reptiles.
